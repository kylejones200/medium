# Brief: Risk Responses That Change Outcomes

Audience
Young professionals and students who need concrete ways to handle project risk.

Goal
Turn the four classic responses into practical moves with clear triggers and examples.

Working Title
Risk responses that actually move the needle

Thesis
Clear triggers lead to better choices. Avoid, mitigate, transfer, and accept work well when tied to conditions, costs, and timelines.

Key Points to Cover

- Avoid. Scope or approach changes that remove the risk entirely. Example involves self financing to avoid bank loan risk, along with tradeoffs.
- Mitigate. Actions that lower probability or impact. Example involves design choices and supplier diversification to reduce material risk.
- Transfer. Movement of financial impact to another party. Example involves insurance and fixed price contracts with acceptance criteria.
- Accept. Conscious choice to monitor without action. Example involves a watch list with review dates and triggers for action.
- Triggers and thresholds. Predefined conditions that move an item from accept to act.
- Cost of response. Simple guidance on spending thresholds to keep total risk cost in check.

Structure
Open with a quick story about interest rates and house builds. Define each response with a trigger, a move, and a tradeoff. Close with a one page response plan for the top five risks.

Materials to Pull From
House build exercise, interest rate lock example, four responses, and discussion of tradeoffs.
