# Brief: Team Peer Evaluation That Actually Helps

Audience: Young career professionals and students working on team projects.
Goal: Help readers give clear, fair peer feedback that leads to better teams and better grades.
Voice: First person, authoritative, approachable.

Working Title
Make Peer Reviews Useful: Clear Notes, Fair Scores, Better Teams

Thesis
A short, honest peer review improves team performance. Clear notes and specific examples guide future behavior and protect the grade for people who carried the work.

Focus Areas
I explain why this exercise matters. I show a simple way to prepare notes during the project. I show how to write scores and comments that match what happened. I close with a compact method for resolving gaps.

Materials to Pull From
Your reminder that you do not see the private team dynamic. The need for candid input. The request for each person to submit their own evaluation. The schedule around the final week.
