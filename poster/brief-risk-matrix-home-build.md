# Brief: Building a Risk Matrix With a House Build Example

Audience
Young career professionals and students learning practical risk management.

Goal
Help readers build a simple risk matrix and act on the top items using a relatable house build example.

Working Title
Building a risk matrix that actually guides action

Thesis
A light structure turns vague worries into specific choices. Probability and impact scores highlight the few risks that matter, and clear responses move the project forward.

Key Points to Cover
The house build example makes risk concrete and memorable.

- Definitions. A risk has not happened. An issue has happened. Positive and negative risks both exist.
- Risk breakdown structure. Categories such as legal, finance, materials, and permits organize thinking.
- Scoring. Probability and impact on 0 to 1 scales create comparable numbers.
- Matrix. A simple grid or chart surfaces the top five items.
- Responses. Avoid, mitigate, transfer, or accept each top risk.
- Example. Interest rate lock reduces financing risk without guesswork.
- Registry. A short, living list captures new risks and lessons from other projects.

Structure
Open with the house scenario. Define the key terms. Walk through scoring and the matrix. Show a quick response plan for the top five. Close with a compact checklist.

Materials to Pull From
Your in class exercise on house build risks, the four risk responses, the definition of risk versus issue, and the interest rate lock example.
